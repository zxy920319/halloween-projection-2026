# Camera mounting checklist

- [ ] Camera optical path through the open window has no glass, film, screen,
  mesh, raindrops, or frame obstruction.
- [ ] Tripod stands on stable indoor flooring, not an unstable sill.
- [ ] Camera base and thread compatibility were physically verified.
- [ ] Camera, tripod, USB connector, and cables have strain relief and
  fall/tip protection.
- [ ] Window sash cannot strike the camera or pinch its cable.
- [ ] Pi supply follows Raspberry Pi requirements; cable route does not create
  a trip hazard.
- [ ] RGB covers feet and head in nighttime conditions, with projector on.
- [ ] Outdoor exposure, rain, condensation, temperature, and direct sunlight
  risks are mitigated; Astra is not assumed weatherproof.
- [ ] An adult supervises the activity; projection is not aimed into eyes.
